About Me

I am a certified cybersecurity professional and AI engineer with extensive experience protecting critical infrastructure using both traditional security methods and cutting-edge machine learning. My approach fuses offensive security expertise with AI to build defenses that are proactive, adaptive, and automated.

I design and deploy AI-powered security systems—from LLM-driven code auditors to real-time anomaly detection pipelines—that catch threats before they escalate. My work sits at the intersection of cybersecurity and artificial intelligence, an area I believe defines the next decade of the industry.
